01 — About

Quality is an engineering discipline, not a gate at the end

I have spent eleven years on one through-line: making sure complex, high-stakes systems actually do what they claim. That started with open-source QA at Red Hat, ran through seven years at Jumio building and then leading quality for an identity-verification platform, and most recently went into an AI agentic system at Vertice Technology.

My work has moved up the stack over time — from writing service, integration, end-to-end and load tests, to leading testers across several business teams, to acting as engineering lead for the Data Extraction domain. What stayed constant is the habit of thinking a few steps forward: where this will break, who will be blocked, what we will wish we had measured.

The problem I find most interesting now is testing systems that are non-deterministic by design. Agents, tool calls and model outputs do not fit the assertions I spent a decade writing, and working out what “correct” even means for them is the part of the job I enjoy most.
